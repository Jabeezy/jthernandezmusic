About JT

JT Hernandez
"Life Lover. Storyteller. Whatever it takes."

JT Hernandez is a rapper, voice-over talent, and show host who came up in the Bay Area and has been grinding ever since, through Las Vegas and settling into Huntington Beach. A true West Coast artist through and through.

His music blends melodic hooks with genuine lyricism, drawing from real life, real journeys, and real connections. With tracks like Spirited Away (feat. Son Broku) and the latest single Running Back accumulating thousands of streams, JT is carving his lane one record at a time.

Beyond the mic, JT's voice talent and show hosting work reflect the same energy, authentic, engaging, and impossible to ignore. A Minority Share Owner and life lover, he shows up fully in everything he does.
